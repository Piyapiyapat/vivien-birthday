Birthday Wishes in Blue

— Written for Vivien 💙

▾

Here is my wish for you: May you always find that shade of blue that makes you feel most like yourself — the blue of morning sky, the blue of the deep sea, the blue of everything still waiting to be explored. Happy Birthday, Vivien.

Blue

— Anonymous

▾

There is a color that holds the sky at dawn, the sea at twilight, the quiet between heartbeats. It is the color of depth, of distance, of everything vast and calm. It is the color of you.

What It Means to Love a Color

— Anonymous

▾

It means the sky makes you stop walking. It means the sea makes you forget your name. It means you see blue and something inside you says: yes — that. That is exactly it.

Blue Hour

— Anonymous

▾

That moment between day and night when the whole sky turns entirely blue — not grey, not black, not purple, but a deep and perfect blue that belongs to no one and everyone all at once. Photographers call it the blue hour. I call it my favorite.

Ocean Mind

— Anonymous

▾

Some people carry storms inside them. You carry something quieter — a deep, wide ocean, unhurried and impossibly clear, full of things worth diving into.

For a Girl Who Loves Blue

— Anonymous

▾

May your days be the color of a clear morning sky. May your heart hold the stillness of deep water. May every shade of blue you encounter remind you how rare and beautiful you are.

Sea Song

— Anonymous

▾

The ocean has no beginning, no ending — just blue folding into blue, wave into wave, horizon into sky. To stand at its edge is to understand something about forever.

On the Sea

— John Keats (1817)

▾

It keeps eternal whisperings around Desolate shores, and with its mighty swell Gluts twice ten thousand Caverns, till the spell Of Hecate leaves them their old shadowy sound. Often 'tis in such gentle temper found, That scarcely will the very smallest shell Be mov'd for days from where it sometime fell, When last the winds of Heaven were unbound.

Dover Beach (opening)

— Matthew Arnold (1867)

▾

The sea is calm tonight. The tide is full, the moon lies fair Upon the straits; on the French coast the light Gleams and is gone; the cliffs of England stand, Glimmering and vast, out in the tranquil bay. Come to the window, sweet is the night-air!
